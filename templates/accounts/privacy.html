Privacy policy

This policy describes what SplitMate stores about you and who can see it. SplitMate does not sell your information.

What we store

An account holds your name, username, email address, password (stored as a hash, not as the password you type), theme and currency preferences, and the time you accepted these terms. If you add a profile picture, that file is stored with the account.

Financial records include expenses, shares, payers, comments, receipts, budgets, recurring bills, informal loans, settlements, and the audit notes that explain later edits, voids, and reversals.

Who can see it

You can see your own records. Friends see the profile and personal balances the app shows between you. Members of a group see that group's expenses, comments, receipts, and balances. People outside those relationships do not.

Receipt files are served only to someone allowed to open that expense.

Email

SplitMate uses your email to verify the address and to send a password-update message when you use Forgot password. That message contains a private link to choose a new password. Verification and password messages are sent only to the address on the account.

How long it stays

Records stay until you delete them, void them, or anonymize the account. Anonymizing replaces your name and email and discards the password. Shared financial rows remain so other people's balances stay correct.
