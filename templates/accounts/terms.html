Terms of use

These terms cover the SplitMate account you create and the records you keep in it. SplitMate is a shared-expense notebook. It is not a bank, wallet, or payment service.

Your account

Create an account with your name, a username, an email address, and a password of at least 6 characters. After the account is saved, sign in with that same username and password. You are responsible for keeping the password private and for the activity on your account.

What you record

You can record group bills, personal expenses, informal loans, and repayments. Enter amounts in the currency of that record. SplitMate checks that shares add up and keeps the earlier version when you edit, void, or reverse something.

Other people in a group can see that group's expenses, comments, receipts, and balances. A friend request or group invitation shares only what the app shows for that relationship.

Money

A repayment note, including cash, UPI, a bank transfer, or Paytm, is your record that money changed hands. SplitMate does not move money, hold a balance at a bank, or confirm that a transfer arrived. A positive balance means that person should receive money. A negative balance means they owe money.

Closing an account

You can deactivate an account or anonymize it. Shared expenses, settlements, and loans stay in place so the other people can still explain their balances. You cannot anonymize an account while you are the only owner of a group that still has other members.
