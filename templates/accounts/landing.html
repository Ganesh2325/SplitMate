How a shared bill becomes a balance

1

Record who paid

One person or several people can pay. Their payments are stored separately from each person's share of the bill.

2

Choose the split

Divide the total equally, by exact amounts, by percentages, by shares, or by item. The server checks that the shares add up.

3

Settle when you are ready

SplitMate can suggest fewer repayments. Nothing is recorded until you confirm the payment yourself.

Balances you can explain

A positive balance means that person should receive money. A negative balance means they owe money. Zero means that pair is settled in that currency.

Group expenses stay with the group. Personal expenses and informal loans are listed on their own, so the same bill is never counted twice.

A plan, then a record

Suggestions show who can pay whom, for how much, and in which group. Recording cash, UPI, a bank transfer, or Paytm saves your note. SplitMate does not move the money.

If a repayment was entered by mistake, reverse it. The original row stays in the history.

History stays on the record

Edits, voids, and reversals change the current balance and keep the earlier version. You can open the expense or repayment that explains any figure.

Receipts, comments, categories, budgets, and recurring bills sit beside that history.
